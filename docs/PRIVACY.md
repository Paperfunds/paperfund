# Paperfund privacy notice draft

The public `/privacy` route is the canonical user-facing draft. It accurately describes the current
implementation: public wallet authentication, simulated competition records, reward/payout records,
the supported terminal hostname and mint context, extension local/session storage, server sessions,
operational logs, and infrastructure categories. It also states that Paperfund does not access seed
phrases/private keys, sign transactions, use advertising/referral/third-party analytics, sell personal
data, or share data for targeted advertising.

Before publication, an authorized operator and counsel must supply/review the publisher legal entity,
privacy and support contacts, jurisdiction-specific rights language, subprocessors, and retention
periods. No support email has been approved; do not invent one. The complete implementation inventory
and Chrome Dashboard disclosure mapping are in [CHROME_WEB_STORE.md](./CHROME_WEB_STORE.md).
